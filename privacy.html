Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Blessed Harvest collects when you take our free journey, visit this page or buy The Blessed Harvest 30-Day Prayer Plan, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • What you give us: your first name and your answers during the free journey; your name, email address and, if requested, phone number at checkout.
  • Payment information: handled directly by our payment processor, Cartpanda. We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Usage information: pages visited, campaign parameters (such as UTM tags) and similar technical data collected through cookies and advertising pixels (for example, Meta/Facebook) to measure our ads.

How we use it

  • To deliver your Plan and send you purchase-related emails.
  • To answer your support requests and process refunds.
  • To understand which pages and ads work best and improve them.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business — such as our checkout and payment processor (Cartpanda), the platform that hosts our journey, and advertising and analytics platforms. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you emails, by writing to us. You can also limit cookies in your browser settings.

Children

Our products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Questions? Email us at blessedharvest4@gmail.com.
